2. Our Privacy Commitment
DeepSleep is a sleep‑aid application that plays soothing sounds to help you fall asleep. It is designed with a zero‑data‑collection​ philosophy:
All sound files are pre‑loaded within the App and played locally on your device.
The App does not​ record, store, or transmit any audio from your environment.
No user data, including sleep patterns, preferences, or personal information, leaves your device.
We operate no servers​ to collect or process your data.
3. Information We Collect
We explicitly state that this App does NOT collect, store, transmit, or share​ any personal information or usage data. Specifically, we do not​ collect:
Personally Identifiable Information:​ name, email address, phone number, etc.
Device & Identification Information:​ device ID, advertising ID, IP address, location data, etc.
Audio Recordings:​ the App does not use the microphone; it only plays pre‑installed sounds.
Usage Data:​ in‑app actions, session duration, feature usage frequency, etc.
Preferences or Settings:​ any volume, timer, or sound selections you make remain only on your device.
4. Permissions
The App requires no special permissions​ (such as microphone, camera, or location). It only uses your device’s built‑in speaker or headphone output to play audio. No network or storage access is needed for normal operation.
5. Data Security
Because we collect no data, there is no risk of your information being exposed through a server breach. Your privacy is fully protected by your device’s own security measures. You are in complete control of your experience.
